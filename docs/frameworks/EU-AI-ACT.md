# AERF × EU AI Act

[← Back to Compliance Overview](../COMPLIANCE.md) · [← Back to README](../../README.md)

This page maps AERF receipts to the EU Artificial Intelligence Act
(Regulation (EU) 2024/1689). AERF is in scope for the *record-keeping
and automatically generated logs* requirements applicable to high-risk
AI systems (Title III). It is out of scope for the policy,
documentation, conformity-assessment, and prohibitive-practices
requirements.

Sources:
- [EU AI Act — Article 12 Record-keeping](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/12/)
- [EU AI Act — Article 19 Automatically Generated Logs](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/19/)
- [EU AI Act — Article 17 Quality Management System](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/17/)

## Primary mappings

### Article 12 — Record-keeping (high-risk AI systems)

- **Requirement (paraphrased; see official text).** "High-risk AI
  systems shall technically allow for the automatic recording of
  events ('logs') over the lifetime of the system. Logging
  capabilities shall enable the recording of events relevant for: (a)
  identifying situations that may result in the high-risk AI system
  presenting a risk within the meaning of Article 79(1) or in a
  substantial modification; (b) facilitating post-market monitoring
  referred to in Article 72; and (c) monitoring the operation of
  high-risk AI systems referred to in Article 26(5)."
- **AERF provides.** Receipts are emitted per agent action over the
  system's lifetime, each carrying `agent`, `action`, `in_policy`,
  `policy_reason`, `observed_at`, `policy_hash`, and an
  `evidence_hash_sha512` over the inline `evidence` payload (see
  [SPEC §4.2](../../SPEC.md#42-required-fields)). Each receipt is
  Ed25519-signed and chain-linked, satisfying the "technically allow
  for the automatic recording of events" obligation with cryptographic
  integrity beyond what the article itself requires.
- **Coverage.** **Partial.** Article 12 also requires logs to be kept
  for at least six months (paragraph 3 in the consolidated text);
  retention is a deployment responsibility, not an AERF property.
  Field-by-field mappings to Annex III paragraph (1)(a) systems
  (period of use, reference database, input data on match,
  identification of natural persons involved in verification) are
  carried inside `evidence` rather than as first-class fields.

### Article 19 — Automatically generated logs (providers and deployers)

- **Requirement (paraphrased).** Providers of high-risk AI systems
  shall keep the logs referred to in Article 12(1), automatically
  generated by their high-risk AI systems, to the extent such logs
  are under their control. Logs shall be kept for a period
  appropriate to the intended purpose of the high-risk AI system, of
  at least six months, unless provided otherwise in applicable Union
  or national law, in particular Union law on the protection of
  personal data.
- **AERF provides.** Per-action signed evidence with independent
  verifiability and chain integrity (SPEC §8). Because verification
  requires only the issuer's public key, retained logs remain
  evidentiary value even when held outside the provider's
  infrastructure (e.g., in a customer's archival store).
- **Coverage.** **Partial.** AERF defines the receipt format; the
  provider remains responsible for the retention duration, secure
  storage, and lawful handling of personal data within receipts (see
  [SPEC §4.5 privacy considerations](../../SPEC.md#45-privacy-considerations)
  and held decision [C-8](../../DECISIONS.md#held-decisions)).

### Article 17 — Quality management system

- **Requirement (paraphrased).** Providers of high-risk AI systems
  shall put a quality management system in place that ensures
  compliance with this Regulation. The system shall include, among
  other elements, "techniques, procedures and systematic actions for
  the design, design control and design verification of the high-risk
  AI system" and "the systems and procedures for data management ...
  for the purposes of placing on the market or putting into service
  high-risk AI systems."
- **AERF provides.** A documented, machine-verifiable evidence
  artifact for runtime decisions taken by the high-risk system. AERF
  is one component of the technical evidence supporting an Article 17
  QMS — specifically the operational logging element.
- **Coverage.** **Partial.** Most of Article 17's elements are
  process and policy controls, not logging.

## Gaps — EU AI Act provisions AERF does not address

- **Title II — Prohibited AI practices.** Out of scope. AERF does not
  enforce or detect prohibited practices; it logs whatever the
  deployment classifies as in-policy or out-of-policy.
- **Articles 9–11 — Risk management, data and data governance,
  technical documentation.** Out of scope; these are programmatic
  controls.
- **Article 13 — Transparency and provision of information to
  deployers.** Out of scope.
- **Article 14 — Human oversight.** AERF can record that a human
  reviewer was involved (via `evidence` content, optional
  `agent_signature`), but does not implement oversight mechanisms.
- **Article 15 — Accuracy, robustness and cybersecurity.** AERF does
  not perform accuracy or robustness testing.
- **Article 16 — Obligations of providers.** Mostly programmatic;
  AERF supports the logging-related obligations in Articles 12 and
  19.
- **Article 26 — Obligations of deployers.** Most deployer
  obligations are organizational; AERF supports the operational
  monitoring portion.
- **Article 50 — Transparency obligations for providers and deployers
  of certain AI systems** (e.g., AI-disclosure to users). Out of
  scope.
- **Article 72 — Post-market monitoring.** AERF logs feed
  post-market monitoring but do not constitute the monitoring program
  itself.

## Auditor verification guide

See [AIUC-1.md#auditor-verification-guide](AIUC-1.md#auditor-verification-guide).
The independent-verifiability property is particularly relevant for
Article 12: an auditor (or notified body, or market-surveillance
authority) can verify any sample receipt directly using only the
provider's public key.
