# AERF × ISO/IEC 42001

[← Back to Compliance Overview](../COMPLIANCE.md) · [← Back to README](../../README.md)

ISO/IEC 42001 is the management-system standard for artificial
intelligence (AIMS — AI Management System). AERF is in scope for the
*records and monitoring evidence* clauses; it is out of scope for the
organizational, planning, and operational clauses that constitute the
management system itself.

Source: [ISO/IEC 42001 standard page](https://www.iso.org/standard/81230.html).

## Primary mappings

### Clause 9.1 — Monitoring, measurement, analysis and evaluation

- **Requirement (paraphrased).** Determine what needs to be monitored
  and measured, the methods to be used, when monitoring and
  measurement are performed, and when the results are analyzed and
  evaluated. Retain documented information as evidence of results.
- **AERF provides.** Each AERF receipt is documented evidence of one
  monitored event: it identifies what was observed (the `action` and
  `evidence` payload), when (`observed_at`, optionally anchored to an
  RFC 3161 trusted timestamp per
  [SPEC §11](../../SPEC.md#11-timestamp-anchoring-locked-decision-c-11)),
  and the policy decision that fired (`in_policy`, `policy_reason`,
  `policy_hash`). The chain (SPEC §8) preserves ordering across
  receipts.
- **Coverage.** **Partial.** AERF supplies the *retained evidence*
  artifact. The plan that determines *what* to monitor and *when* to
  evaluate sits in the organization's documented AI Management System.

### Clause 10.2 — Nonconformity and corrective action

- **Requirement (paraphrased).** When a nonconformity occurs, react
  to it, evaluate the need for action to eliminate causes, implement
  any action needed, review effectiveness, and **retain documented
  information as evidence of the nature of the nonconformity, action
  taken, and the result of the corrective action.**
- **AERF provides.** Receipts with `in_policy: false` are signed,
  immutable records of nonconformities at the agent-action level.
  `policy_reason` describes the nature; subsequent receipts in the
  chain document the actions that followed. AERF receipts cannot be
  retroactively modified to obscure or revise a recorded
  nonconformity.
- **Coverage.** **Partial.** AERF preserves the evidence record. The
  root-cause analysis, corrective-action workflow, and effectiveness
  review remain organizational.

> **Note on the user-prompted clause 10.1.** Clause 10.1 in ISO/IEC
> 42001 is *Continual improvement* (process), not *Nonconformity*.
> The nonconformity and corrective-action requirements are in
> **clause 10.2**. This page maps AERF to the clause where the
> "documented information as evidence" obligation is normative. See
> [Hicomply's clause-by-clause summary](https://www.hicomply.com/hub/the-core-requirements-of-iso-42001-clauses-4-10)
> for a public reference.

## Auxiliary mappings

- **Clause 7.5 — Documented information.** AERF receipts are a class
  of documented information: they are created with controlled
  identification (`id`, UUIDv4 in v0.1), attribution (`key_id`,
  `agent`), and protection from unintended alteration (Ed25519
  signature). Coverage: **Full** for the per-receipt control of
  documented information; **Partial** for the broader system that
  organizes, retains, and disposes of such documented information.
- **Clause 9.2 — Internal audit.** AERF receipts can serve as
  primary evidence during internal audits of the AI management
  system. Coverage: **Partial** — evidence supply only.
- **Annex A controls (informative).** Where annex A controls touch
  logging or traceability of AI-system operation, AERF satisfies the
  cryptographic-integrity portion. The annex's policy and process
  controls remain out of scope.

## Gaps — ISO/IEC 42001 clauses AERF does not address

- **Clause 4 — Context of the organization.** Out of scope.
- **Clause 5 — Leadership.** Out of scope.
- **Clause 6 — Planning.** Risk assessment and AI objectives are out
  of scope; AERF does not define risk taxonomies.
- **Clause 7 — Support** (resources, competence, awareness,
  communication). Out of scope, except 7.5 above.
- **Clause 8 — Operation.** AI system impact assessment, data
  management, and AI system life-cycle clauses are out of scope; AERF
  records *outcomes*, not the process of design, development, or
  deployment.
- **Clause 10.1 — Continual improvement.** Out of scope (process
  control, not an evidence requirement).
